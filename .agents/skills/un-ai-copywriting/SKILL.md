# Un-AI Copywriting & Anti-Fluff Style Guide

This skill provides an authoritative, zero-compromise editorial framework to remove the "AI smell" from software marketing copy, landing pages, user interface microcopy, and technical communications.

---

## 1. The Banned Lexicon (AI Slop & Corporate Fluff)

Never use these words or their variations. Replace them with concrete, simple human words.

| Banned AI Buzzword | Why It's Banned | What to Use Instead |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seamless / Seamlessly** | Classic AI filler; meaningless abstraction | "Direct," "Automatic," "In one step," or delete entirely |
| **Empower / Empowering** | Overused corporate cliché | "Let," "Help," "Give you," or state the specific capability |
| **Unlock / Unleash** | Video-game hype language | "Access," "Open," "Use," "Turn on" |
| **Elevate / Supercharge** | Generic amplifier; lacks substance | "Improve," "Speed up," "Organize," "Build" |
| **Revolutionize / Transform** | Overclaiming hyperbole | "Replace," "Fix," "Simplify," "Change" |
| **Robust / Holistic** | Vague filler adjective | Be specific about what makes it strong or comprehensive |
| **Tapestry / Delve** | Definitive LLM signature tell | Delete or use "explore," "dig into," "look at" |
| **Testament / Beacon** | Stilted, overly formal prose | "Proof," "Evidence," "Example" |
| **Game-changer / Paradigm shift** | Dated tech marketing jargon | Explain the concrete benefit directly |
| **Harness / Foster** | Pompous, indirect verbs | "Use," "Apply," "Grow," "Build" |
| **Myriad / Plethora** | Artificial vocabulary flexing | "Many," "Dozens of," "Hundreds of," or specify exact numbers |
| **In today's fast-paced...** | Instant landing-page killer | Start immediately with the user's specific problem |
| **It's not just X, it's Y** | Most overused AI contrast trope | State what it is and why it matters directly |

## 2. Syntactic Anti-AI Rules

### Rule 1: Break the Symmetrical Paragraph Rhythm
* **AI Pattern:** Generates uniform 3-to-4 sentence paragraphs with identical cadence.
* **Human Pattern:** Mix sentence lengths drastically. Follow a 20-word explanatory sentence with a 3-word declaration. Use short paragraphs. Use fragments where natural.

### Rule 2: Kill Formulaic Contrastive Clauses
* **Banned:** *"It's not a resume—it's your career operating system."*
* **Banned:** *"Stop managing spreadsheets. Start managing your future."*
* **Human:** *"Keep your licenses, case studies, and renewal hours in one place you own. Share a single link instead of emailing five PDFs."*

### Rule 3: Kill the Synthetic Tricolon (Three-Word Slogans)
* **Banned:** *"Capture. Verify. Showcase."* / *"Fast. Secure. Compliant."*
* **Human:** Explain the actual flow with causal clarity: *"Upload your certificate, verify it directly against the state board, and add it to your public portfolio."*

### Rule 4: Eliminate "Process Bleed" and Meta-Language
* Do not describe what the text is doing (*"In this section, we explore..."* or *"Here is a breakdown of why this matters"*).
* Jump directly into the information without conversational warm-ups.

## 3. The "Concrete Practitioner Pain" Test

AI writing lives in the clouds (*"optimizing your holistic professional narrative"*). Human copy lives in the dirt (*"finding your 2023 BLS card two hours before a hospital audit"*).

When writing for any professional persona:
1. **Identify the exact physical or digital annoyance:** Re-typing license numbers into hospital portals, hunting for CE certificates in messy downloads folders, sending case studies that get blocked by email attachment limits.
2. **Use the real industry vocabulary:** Mention actual credential boards (ANCC, NREMT, PMI, State Boards), real file formats, real renewal cycles, and real shift/project realities.
3. **Show, Don't Declare:** Instead of saying *"Our verification engine provides unprecedented trust,"* write *"State board verification badges resolve directly against official registry databases with zero guesswork."*

---

## 4. UI & Visual Anti-AI Standards

1. **Retire the Sparkles (`✨` / `Sparkles`) Epidemic:** Do not put sparkle icons on every badge pill. Use icons that actually describe the domain (e.g. `ShieldCheck`, `Building2`, `FileText`, `CheckCircle2`).
2. **High-Craft Editorial Design Over Glowing Cards:** Prefer high-contrast, clean typography, crisp border lines, and realistic UI previews over generic purple/neon gradient cards with blurry dropshadows.
3. **Asymmetry in Layouts:** Don't force every section into a 3-box card grid with 3 bullet points each. Use varied layouts: two-column comparison tables, interactive tabs, detailed document cards, and data-dense snippets.

---

## 5. The 3-Pass Editorial Process

When un-AI-ing an existing draft:
* **Pass 1 (Vocabulary Sweep):** Search for and eradicate every word in the Banned Lexicon.
* **Pass 2 (Syntax & Rhythm):** Remove all "Not X, but Y" constructions. Break up monotonic sentence lengths.
* **Pass 3 (Grounding & Reality):** Add at least two specific, tangible details, numbers, or workflow scenarios that only someone who understands the job would know.
